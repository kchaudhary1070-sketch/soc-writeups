- **Platform:** TryHackMe
- **Path:** Cyber Security 101
- **Time taken:** 10 minutes 
- **Tasks completed:** Task 1 Introduction, Task 2 Phase 1: An Attack Begins, Task 3 Phase 2: Stopping the Attack, Task 4 Phase 3: Investigating the Attacker, Task 5 Phase 4: Submitting Your Report

## What I learned
- In Phase 1, I noticed a brute force attack on the login page, where the attacker tried thousands of passwords.
- In Phase 2, I stopped the attack by blocking the account.
- In Phase 3, threat intelligence helped me find details about the attacker, like the timing and the attacker group.
## Key takeaway
- Login pages need protection, such as blocking repeated failed attempts, so attackers cannot keep guessing passwords.
