- Task 3: Find Hidden Pages

## What I learned
- A hidden page is not secure just because its URL is hard to find.
- Attackers can discover hidden pages and use them to gain access.
- Admin pages must have access control, such as a login.
## Key takeaway
- Security means controlling who can access something, not just hiding it.
- "Require login" was the most effective option, because guessing a URL or moving the page to another server does not really secure it.
